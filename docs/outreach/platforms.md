# Where tradespeople actually are — channel research

Researched 2026-07-24. Reach figures are **as reported by the sources at the
bottom**, not measured by us. Useful for ranking channels, not for forecasting.

## The finding that should change the plan

The instinct is to start with Reddit because it's familiar. But the **established
trade forums are where the seniors are**, and seniors are the scarce resource.
Reddit skews younger and more apprentice-heavy — the side we can already fill.

Forums are also far less hostile to a genuine introduction than Reddit, provided
you contribute first. Slower, less glamorous, better bet.

## Notes per channel

**Contractor Talk** — the single highest-value target on this list, because it's
where shop owners are, and shop owners have apprentices. Multi-trade, so one
account covers all three verticals. Expect to spend real time contributing before
mentioning anything.

**Terry Love** — reported hourly activity, which for a forum is genuinely busy.
Mixed pro/DIY means the pros there are already used to answering beginners, i.e.
pre-qualified for what we're asking.

**Mike Holt** — the most expert-heavy venue and the least tolerant of anything
promotional. High risk, high value. Only worth approaching once Krish can post
under his own name and answer follow-ups competently.

**Facebook groups** — the admin's opinion is the only one that matters. Join,
comment helpfully for a week, then message the admin. Post as a person, not a
page; page posts get throttled and read as advertising.

**Reddit** — see `reddit-facebook-templates.md` for the ban-risk rules. Summary:
check each rules tab, use an aged account, message mods before naming the
platform, and never cross-post identical copy.

**TikTok / Instagram** — the interview format is native here and doubles as the
reason to contact people. TikTok's own trades push makes the algorithm friendlier
than expected for this content.

## Trade weighting

Lead with **electrical and HVAC**. Seeded content is strongest there, and
licensing makes those juniors most acutely aware of what they don't know — which
is the itch the product scratches.
