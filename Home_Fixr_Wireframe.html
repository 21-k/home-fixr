The Concept (one paragraph)

Home Fixr is a social community platform that pairs senior trade experts — plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians — with new entrants to the trade. New graduates come to learn the unwritten knowledge of the work (pricing, customer relationships, job-site judgment, code questions) directly from people who have been doing it for decades. Optional job collaboration lets members team up when an extra hand or extra expertise is needed.

Scope is intentionally narrow: it is a community, not a marketplace, not a lead-gen service, and not a business-in-a-box.

Asked by Emma R. · Apprentice Plumber · 2 hours ago · Tagged: Plumbing, Licensing

Senior pro ✓ Accepted answer

Easy script: "I'd love to help you with that but it's outside my license — if I do it and something goes wrong, your insurance won't cover it. Let me put you in touch with someone I trust." Then actually have someone to refer. Customers respect this every single time. The ones who push back after that aren't customers you want.
