# Home Fixr — Project Brief

**Last updated:** May 25, 2026
**Contributors:** Krish, Hassan, Gaurav

---

## Vision

Home Fixr is a **social community platform** for the skilled trades. It connects **senior experts** — experienced plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, and other tradespeople — with **new entrants** to the trade, so juniors can learn from those who have already walked the path. The platform is intentionally scoped as a community, not a marketplace: members come for mentorship, guidance, and the occasional opportunity to collaborate on jobs when extra hands or expertise are needed.

## What Home Fixr Is (and Is Not)

**Home Fixr is:**
- A social space where senior tradespeople share knowledge, advice, and lived experience with newcomers
- A mentorship network where juniors can ask questions, learn the unwritten rules of the trade, and find guidance on building their careers
- A lightweight collaboration layer for members who want to partner on jobs together when the work calls for it

**Home Fixr is not:**
- A lead-generation marketplace like Angie or TaskRabbit
- A "business-in-a-box" with invoicing, payments, or back-office tooling
- A certification or verification service

Keeping the scope narrow — community and collaboration only — keeps the platform simple to build, lighter on liability, and more authentic to the relationships it's trying to foster.

---

## Why This Matters

New entrants to the trades often face a steep learning curve that has little to do with the technical skills they learned in school: how to talk to customers, how to price work, how to handle difficult job sites, how to know when to walk away. Most of that knowledge gets passed informally, from one tradesperson to another, and is hard to access for someone without family or community connections already in the field. Home Fixr exists to make that mentorship accessible at scale.

---

## Strategic Positioning

### Differentiation from Existing Platforms
Existing platforms such as Angie (formerly Angie's List) and TaskRabbit primarily monetize by charging service providers for leads. Home Fixr is deliberately positioned upstream of all that — focused on the human relationships, mentorship, and community that help tradespeople succeed long before any lead would matter.

## Session Summary — May 25, 2026

### Discussion Overview
Hassan and Krish, along with Gaurav, discussed the development of Home Fixr. The session explored what the platform should and shouldn't try to do. After weighing the complexity of operational tooling (invoicing, payments, certification) against the time and resources available, the team narrowed scope to a social community platform centered on senior-to-junior mentorship and optional job collaboration. The session concluded with action items for Krish covering market research, partner identification, and wireframe development.

### 1. Platform Concept
- Home Fixr will be a **social community platform** connecting senior trade experts with new entrants.
- Core interactions: senior tradespeople guide juniors; juniors ask questions and learn; members can optionally collaborate on jobs together.
- Scope is intentionally kept narrow to preserve focus and minimize operational complexity.

### 2. Challenges and Considerations
- **Built-in business tools** (invoicing, payments) were considered and set aside — too complex for the current scope and require a financial/tax operation to support.
- **Trade-certified provider verification** was discussed and deprioritized; verification will not be a core platform feature.
- **Liability exposure** remains a consideration even as a community platform; legal counsel should still be engaged to review terms of service, member conduct policies, and any liability around job collaboration.

### 3. Phased Execution Plan
- **Month 1:** Market research, partner identification, and establishing legal structure.
- **Initial build:** Web app version first, with possible expansion to mobile (Android) later.
- **Funding reality check:** Seed funding is unrealistic in the initial months without an MVP. Focus on building the community first.

### 4. Wireframe and Partner Collaboration
- Hassan shared a wireframe template for Krish to use as a starting point.
- Krish to research New Jersey organizations and vocational schools as early community partners and recruiting pipelines for both senior mentors and junior members.
